Flip TV tune-in

Version 3: analog static (with hiss), and the bat now opens its wings on a win. Tap a word to tune the TV to it, tap Correct for its win move. Switch layers off to judge how much is too much.

What changed

  1. TV. Snow is drawn like an old set: fine streaky grain, a dark sync bar rolling down, the picture rolling and tearing before it locks, and a short hiss. About one second.
  2. Life. The bat mostly sulks, and every few seconds its eyes pop open for a peek. The taxi stays movement only.
  3. Win. The bat wakes up, throws its wings open, flaps twice and folds back grinning (one extra Canva frame). The taxi drives off and skids back (no extra frame). The robot and voice join here later.
